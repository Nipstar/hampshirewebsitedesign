Cost guide

What a website costs in Hampshire

A Hampshire website costs whatever its scope costs, and a few things drive that on any quote. Andy's fixed prices are £600 for five pages and £995 for eight, with extra pages at £125.

Number of pages

Every page has to be written, designed and tested, so page count moves a quote more than almost anything else.

Who writes the words

Some prices assume you supply all the text. Ask whether writing is included. In a build from Andy, it is.

Search set-up

Technical SEO and schema may be built in or sold as an extra. Every Antek build includes them.

What happens after launch

Hosting, updates and support might sit inside a price or arrive later as a bill. Here, hosting and care is £10 a month.

Bespoke features

Booking systems, shops and integrations add work. Get those priced as separate lines so you can see what each one costs.

Who is doing it

An agency carries more staff and overheads than one designer. That can be worth paying for on a big project, and unnecessary on a small one.

The honest comparison is the total over a year: the build, the hosting and any monthly fee you are signing up to. These are Andy's prices only, and other Hampshire designers set their own.

A checklist for any builder

What every Hampshire business website needs

Six things, roughly in the order a customer notices them. Use them to test your current site or any proposal you are given.

1. What you do, straight away

A visitor should know your trade and whether you can help them before they scroll.

2. Where you work

Name the towns you cover, whether that means Romsey and Eastleigh or the whole county, so people and search engines can see the fit.

3. A fast phone experience

Most local searches happen on a phone, so the site has to load quickly and work under a thumb.

4. One obvious way to get in touch

A tappable number and a short form, repeated where people need them, with enquiries going to your inbox.

5. Evidence you are real

Your name, your address, genuine reviews and photographs of actual work do more than any slogan.

6. Set up for search

Clear page titles, schema and Google Search Console from launch day, so your site can be read properly.

Five questions

How to choose a web designer in Hampshire

Ask any designer these five questions, Andy included, and lay the answers side by side.

Is the price fixed and written down?

An estimate can grow. Look for a figure tied to a list of pages and features. Andy's are published on this page.

Who will actually do the work?

Ask for the name of the person building your site. With Antek Automation it is always Andy Norman.

What will I pay after launch?

Get every monthly charge in writing before you commit. Andy's hosting and care is £10 a month.

Who owns the site?

Some firms keep control of a site if you leave them, so ask before you sign. Andy's answer is short: You own the site once it is paid for.

Can I open sites you have built?

Screenshots prove little. Use the real thing and ask which are paying clients. Andy shows one live client site and labels the rest as demo builds.

You own the site once it is paid for.

Yes, every time. Andy Norman takes the first call, writes and builds the site, and answers when you ring afterwards.

Do I need a designer in my own town?

Not necessarily. What counts is whether they understand the area you serve and pick up the phone. Most of Andy's work is done by phone and video.

Is SEO included in the price?

Technical SEO and schema come with every build. Ongoing local SEO is a separate service from £497 a month, and you do not need it to launch.

Which is right for me, five pages or eight?

Five pages suits a sole trader with a few services. Eight gives a firm with several services or towns room to give each its own page.

Can I add AI to my website later?

Yes. An AI chatbot starts from £297 to set up and an AI voice receptionist from £497, each with its own monthly fee, and you can add either after launch.

Should I use an agency or a single designer?

It depends on the job. A larger agency suits a big project that needs several specialists. A single designer suits most local firms that want one contact and a known price.
